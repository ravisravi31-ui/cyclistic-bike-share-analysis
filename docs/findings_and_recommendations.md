# Findings and recommendations

## Key findings

### Customer mix
- Member trips form the majority of 2019 rides.
- Casual riders form a smaller share of total rides but have a much longer average ride duration.

### Ride duration
- Casual average ride length: **39.43 minutes**
- Member average ride length: **12.93 minutes**
- **72.6%** of valid rides are under 20 minutes.

### Timing
- Thursday is the highest-volume day.
- August is the highest-volume month.
- Q3 is the highest-volume quarter.
- 17:00 is the highest-volume hour.

### Weekly behavior
- Casual riders have a larger weekend share of usage than Members.
- Average ride length increases on weekends for both groups.

### Stations and routes
The most frequently used starting/ending stations and station-to-station routes were identified as supporting analysis.

## Recommendations

### 1. Test seasonal and weekend membership offers
Use periods with stronger Casual demand as test windows for membership messaging. Track exposure, sign-up and conversion.

### 2. Test messaging around longer rides
Casual riders take much longer rides on average. Test whether membership-value messaging resonates with customers whose usage resembles longer or more leisure-oriented trips.

### 3. Test high-demand location/time touchpoints
Test messaging at high-traffic locations and during late-afternoon demand periods, with clear attribution and conversion measurement.

## Limitations

The analysis identifies behavioral differences but does not establish why those differences occur or prove that a specific marketing intervention will increase membership conversion. Campaign, pricing and conversion data would be needed for causal testing.
